## T—Together time

Protected, undistracted time—even ten minutes—changes everything.

## A—Acknowledgment

Being seen and named matters more than being fixed.

## L—Listening

Listening to understand, not to reply.

## K—Kindness in small moments

The small gestures compound into a felt sense of safety.
